06 · Summary

What did we discover?

Key findings

  • Egypt’s long-term average has climbed by about +0.36 °C per decade since 2000.
  • Summer heat is rising faster than winter, with a stronger seasonal signal.
  • Delta and canal governorates warm fastest, while the hottest regions are often not the fastest-warming.
  • Projected warming through 2035 suggests the country remains on an upward trend.

Limits of the data

  • Monthly NASA POWER averages, not heatwaves.
  • 2025 excluded because the year is incomplete.
  • Cairo daily and monthly sources diverge after 2010.
  • Linear forecast is simplified.
