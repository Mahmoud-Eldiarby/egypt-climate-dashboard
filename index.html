A note on interpretation — This is an observed temperature trend for one selected location, not proof of a specific cause and not a proxy for all of Egypt. Monthly averages describe climate patterns; they do not reveal individual heatwaves or daily extremes. The dashboard labels the fitted trend stable when its slope is within ±0.01 °C per year.

About the project

Making climate
data understandable.

This project investigates historical temperature changes in Egypt using NASA POWER climate data. By collecting, processing, and visualizing historical temperature observations, it aims to identify long-term patterns and make climate data easier to understand.
